Installing these. Android blocks apps from outside the Play Store until you say otherwise: open the APK, and when the phone objects, allow installs from whichever app you opened it with (usually the browser or your files app). These are debug builds — they are unsigned for release, so the phone will warn you, and that warning is correct. Install them because you know where they came from, not because the phone was reassured.
