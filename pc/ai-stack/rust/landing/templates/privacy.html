How this site treats your data

Plain answers, because this is a family-run site and there is nothing to hide behind. Last updated {{ year }}.

Who runs this

This site and everything on it run on hardware owned by Site Owner, in his home. There is no company behind it, no ad network, and nothing here is sold, rented, or shared for money — data least of all.

What is collected, and why

  • Accounts. If you have a sign-in here, the site stores your name, email address, a securely hashed password (never the password itself), which apps you may use, and your chosen page theme. That is the entire account record.
  • The sign-in cookie. Signing in sets one cookie that proves you are you. It is signed, it expires on its own, and it is the only cookie this site sets. There are no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party cookies of any kind.
  • Server logs. Like nearly every web server, this one keeps ordinary access logs (address, page, time) for troubleshooting. They stay on the same hardware and are looked at when something breaks.

Photos and family material

  • Photo libraries are private to each person. Nobody else in the family can see your photos, and they are never public.
  • Face grouping and photo tagging are done by software running on this same home hardware. No photo is sent to any outside AI service for analysis.
  • The public memorial pages (such as The First Voice) show only material the family chose to publish, and are marked so search engines do not index them.

Outside services this site does use

A few things necessarily involve someone else, and here is the whole list:

  • Certificates — Let's Encrypt / ZeroSSL issue the HTTPS certificates. They see only the site's hostnames.
  • Phone calls — the phone features use Twilio, a telephone carrier. When you call, Twilio handles the call the way any carrier does, including your number, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Photos — only for a family member who chooses to connect their own Google account, to copy their own photos here. The connection is theirs, made by them, and can be disconnected any time.
  • Email — password-reset codes are sent through an ordinary email provider when requested.

What is never done

  • No selling, sharing, or trading of any personal data. Ever.
  • No advertising, no analytics services, no tracking pixels.
  • No AI training on your material, and no sending your photos, files, or messages to outside AI services.

Your data, your call

Want something corrected or deleted — an account, photos, anything? Ask Jason directly, and it is done. There is no form, no waiting period, and no fine print: it is his hardware, and your data on it stays yours.

This page covers example.org and its related subdomains (photos, status, studio, peewee, and kin), which all run on the same household hardware under the same rules.
