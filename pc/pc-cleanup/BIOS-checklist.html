SuperNova BIOS checklist

Gigabyte B650 AORUS ELITE AX · Ryzen 9 9900X · BIOS FB8 (2025-11-19) · read from Windows on 2026-11-04

Getting in: restart and tap Delete while the Gigabyte logo shows. Press F2 for Advanced Mode if it opens in Easy Mode. When you're done, F10 saves and restarts.

Updating the BIOS? Do it first (Q-Flash, from a USB stick), because a flash resets everything below. It's optional: only do it if Gigabyte's support page lists a version newer than FB8.

Change these

SettingWhereSet to / why
☐AC BACKSettings → Platform PowerAlways On
The PC turns itself back on after a power cut (9/13 took both machines down).
☐Initial Display OutputSettings → IO PortsPCIe 1 Slot
Only if your monitor plugs into the RTX 4070.
☐Integrated GraphicsSettings → IO PortsDisabled
Gives back about 2.4 GB of RAM the AMD integrated graphics is holding. Same condition: monitor on the 4070.

Optional

SettingWhereSet to / why
☐ErPSettings → Platform PowerDisabled (needed for Wake on LAN)
☐Wake on LANSettings → Platform PowerEnabled (the NUC can wake the PC)
